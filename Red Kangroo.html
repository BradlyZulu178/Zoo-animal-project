Info on the Red Kangaroo

Website all about them and where info came from
  • Basic Information - Name: Red Kangaroo / Conservation: Least Concern
  • Full Scientific Classification -
    • Kingdom: Animalia
    • Phylum: Chordata
    • Subphylum: Vertebrata
    • Class: Mammalia
    • Subclass: Theria
    • Order: Diprotodontia
    • Suborder: Macropodiformes
    • Family: Macropodidae
    • Genus: Osphranter
    • Species: Osphranter rufus
  • Physical Profile - Is 6 feet tall frmales are smaller size then males they have Large body, powerful hind legs, long tail; males are reddish-brown and females are usually blue-gray.
  • Habitat & Geography - Are in Grasslands, deserts, shrublands, and open plains inside interior of Australia.
  • Ecology & Behavior -
    • Feeding and Diet: Mainly grasses and other vegetation
    • Behaviors and Adaptations: Hops using powerful hind legs and uses its tail for balance
    • Communication: Uses body movements, sounds, and physical signals
    • Life History & Breeding Behaviors: Gives birth to a very small young that develops in the mother’s pouch
    • Predators: Dingoes, eagles, and humans can prey on them or their young
