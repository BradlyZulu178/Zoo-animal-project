The Red Tailed Hawk

Info on the Red Tailed Hawk

Website all about them and where info came from
  • Basic Information - Name: Red Tailed Hawk / Conservation: Least Concern
  • Full Scientific Classification -
    • Kingdom: Animalia
    • Phylum: Chordata
    • Subphylum: Vertebrata
    • Class: Aves
    • Subclass: Neornithes
    • Order: Falconiformes
    • Suborder: Accipitres
    • Family: Accipitridae
    • Genus: Buteo
    • Species: Buteo jamaicensis
  • Physical Profile - 18-26 inches long with a reddish brown tail, broad wings, pale underside.
  • Habitat & Geography - Habitats are grasslands, forest, deserts, farms, and open areas in most of North America and parts of central America.
  • Ecology & Behavior -
    • Feeding and Diet: Mice, rats, rabbits, birds, and reptiles
    • Behaviors and Adaptations: Soars above open areas and hunts from high perches
    • Communication: Loud, high-pitched calls
    • Life History & Breeding Behaviors: Builds nests in trees and usually raises several young
    • Predators: Great horned owls and other predators may attack eggs or young
